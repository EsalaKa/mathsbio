Hospital-capacity prediction

Epidemic models can be connected to hospital demand by translating infections or symptomatic cases into admissions, occupancy and critical-care requirements.

Occupancy dynamics

If \(A(t)\) is the admission rate and patients leave at per-capita rate \(\delta\), a simple occupancy model is

\[\frac{dH}{dt}=A(t)-\delta H(t).\]

For capacity \(C\), the decision-relevant event is

\[H(t)>C.\]

Uncertain demand

When epidemic trajectories or clinical parameters are uncertain, the probability of exceeding capacity can be more informative than one projected occupancy curve:

\[P\left(\max_{0\le t\le T}H(t)>C\right).\]

Use

Such models can compare intervention timings, staffing requirements and surge-capacity scenarios.

Key idea. Capacity planning is naturally an exceedance-risk problem when future epidemic and hospital demand are uncertain.
