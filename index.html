Learning Path

Begin with the mathematical ideas needed for modelling and progress towards advanced mathematical biology.

01

Mathematical Foundations

Calculus, differential equations, linear algebra, probability, statistics and numerical methods.

02

Mathematical Modelling

Variables, parameters, rates, assumptions, model construction, scaling and model interpretation.

03

Core Biological Models

Population dynamics, epidemiology, ecology, biochemical systems, physiology and evolution.

04

Stochastic & Spatial Modelling

Markov chains, stochastic differential equations, Gillespie simulation, moments, diffusion and spatial models.

05

Data & Computation

Python, simulation, parameter estimation, uncertainty, model fitting, statistics and machine learning.

06

Advanced Topics & Applications

Networks, pattern formation, decision making, forecasting and worked mathematical-biology applications.

About MathsBio

MathsBio is designed as a comprehensive and progressive guide to mathematical biology. Topics will be developed from their mathematical foundations, connected to biological meaning, and supported by models and computational examples.
