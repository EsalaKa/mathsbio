Degree distributions

The degree distribution describes how the numbers of connections are distributed across nodes.

If \(P(k)\) is the probability that a randomly chosen node has degree \(k\), then

\[\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}P(k)=1.\]

The mean degree is

\[\langle k\rangle=\sum_k kP(k).\]

Heterogeneity

A narrow distribution means most nodes have similar numbers of contacts. A broad distribution means some nodes have many more contacts than others.

This heterogeneity can substantially alter spreading processes and epidemic thresholds.

Key idea. The degree distribution summarises variation in connectivity across a network.
