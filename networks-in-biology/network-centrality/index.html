Network centrality

Centrality measures attempt to quantify the structural importance of nodes, but different definitions capture different meanings of importance.

Degree centrality

For an undirected graph, degree centrality is based on \(k_i=\sum_jA_{ij}\). It identifies nodes with many direct neighbours.

Betweenness centrality

Betweenness measures how often a node lies on shortest paths between other nodes, identifying potential bridges between regions of a network.

Eigenvector centrality

Eigenvector centrality assigns high scores to nodes connected to other high-scoring nodes. In vector form,

\[Ax=\lambda x.\]

Interpretation

No centrality measure is universally best. The appropriate measure depends on the biological process being studied.

Key idea. Centrality is process-dependent: being highly connected, being a bridge and being connected to influential nodes are different structural properties.
