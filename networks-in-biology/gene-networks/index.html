Gene networks

A gene network represents regulatory relationships among genes and their products.

Nodes may represent genes, mRNA or proteins. Directed edges can represent activation or inhibition.

Network and dynamical descriptions

The graph records which regulatory interactions exist. A dynamical model then specifies how strongly those interactions affect expression through Boolean rules, differential equations or stochastic reaction models.

Feedback motifs

Positive feedback can support switching and multiple stable expression states. Negative feedback can stabilise expression and can also participate in oscillatory regulatory circuits.

Key idea. A gene network describes regulatory architecture; dynamical equations determine how that architecture produces changing expression levels.
