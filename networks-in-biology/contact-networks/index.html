Contact networks

A contact network represents potentially infectious contacts between individuals or groups.

Each individual is a node. An edge indicates that transmission is possible through the type of contact represented by the model.

Degree

The degree of node \(i\) is the number of its neighbours:

\[k_i=\sum_j A_{ij}.\]

Individuals with many contacts may have more opportunities both to acquire and to transmit infection.

Static and temporal networks

A static network assumes connections are fixed during the modelled period. A temporal network allows edges to appear, disappear or change through time.

Key idea. Contact networks replace homogeneous mixing with explicit information about which individuals can interact.
