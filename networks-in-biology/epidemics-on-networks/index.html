Epidemics on networks

Network epidemic models allow transmission only along specified contacts rather than assuming every individual mixes equally with the population.

Node states

Each node can have an epidemic state such as susceptible, infectious or recovered. If susceptible node \(i\) has \(m_i\) infectious neighbours and transmission occurs independently along each infectious edge at rate \(\tau\), its total infection hazard is

\[\lambda_i=\tau m_i.\]

Recovery may occur at rate \(\gamma\).

Effect of topology

Degree heterogeneity, clustering, communities and highly connected nodes can change outbreak probability, epidemic size and speed.

Key idea. In network epidemics, infection pressure is determined by an individual's infectious neighbours rather than only by the total infectious population.
