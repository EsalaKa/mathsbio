Data and Parameter Estimation

Mathematical models become useful for real biological systems when their variables and parameters are connected carefully to observations. Begin with biological data and preparation, then learn how parameters are estimated before studying uncertainty, identifiability, sensitivity, calibration, validation and comparison between competing models.
