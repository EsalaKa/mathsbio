Protein dynamics

Protein dynamics models describe how protein abundance changes through production, degradation, modification and molecular interactions.

A basic production–degradation model is

\[\frac{dP}{dt}=k_p m-\delta_pP,\]

where mRNA \(m\) drives protein production and \(\delta_p\) controls protein degradation.

Characteristic timescale

For first-order degradation, the characteristic timescale is approximately \(1/\delta_p\). Slowly degraded proteins retain the effect of earlier production for longer.

Key idea. Protein abundance is dynamic: it reflects both current production and the history of removal and modification.
