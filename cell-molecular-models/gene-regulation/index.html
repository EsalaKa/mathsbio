Gene regulation

Gene regulation controls when and how strongly a gene is expressed. Mathematical models describe the balance between production and degradation of gene products.

A simple model is

\[\frac{dm}{dt}=\alpha-\delta m,\]

where \(m\) is mRNA, \(\alpha\) is its production rate and \(\delta\) its degradation rate.

Regulated production

Production can depend on a transcription factor through an activation or repression function, often represented using Hill functions.

Key idea. Gene regulation can be viewed dynamically as controlled molecular production competing with degradation.
