← Cell and Molecular Biology Models

Cell signalling

Cell signalling models describe how cells detect external or internal signals and convert them into molecular responses.

A simple receptor-binding process is

\[L+R\rightleftharpoons LR,\]

where ligand \(L\) binds receptor \(R\) to form a complex \(LR\).

Signal transduction

Receptor activation can trigger downstream reaction cascades. Mathematical models study amplification, saturation, feedback, adaptation and the timescale of the response.

Key idea. A signalling pathway transforms an input signal into a dynamic cellular output through interacting molecular reactions.
