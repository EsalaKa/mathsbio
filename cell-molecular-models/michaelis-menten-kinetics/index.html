Michaelis–Menten kinetics

Michaelis–Menten kinetics describes how the rate of many enzyme-catalysed reactions depends on substrate concentration.

\[\boxed{v=\frac{V_{\max}[S]}{K_M+[S]}.}\]

At low substrate concentration the rate rises approximately linearly. At high concentration the enzyme becomes saturated and the rate approaches \(V_{\max}\).

Half-saturation

When \([S]=K_M\),

\[v=\frac{V_{\max}}{2}.\]

Thus \(K_M\) identifies the substrate concentration giving half the maximum rate.

Key idea. The Michaelis–Menten curve rises with substrate concentration but saturates because enzyme availability is finite.
