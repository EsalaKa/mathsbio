Cell-cycle models

Cell-cycle models describe the regulatory processes that move a cell through growth, DNA replication, preparation and division.

Mathematically, cell-cycle control can be represented using coupled nonlinear differential equations for regulatory proteins such as cyclins and cyclin-dependent kinases.

Why nonlinear dynamics matter

Feedback can create thresholds, switches and oscillations. These behaviours help explain how a cell makes robust transitions between cell-cycle stages.

Key idea. Cell-cycle progression is not simply a clock; it emerges from interacting molecular regulators and feedback loops.
