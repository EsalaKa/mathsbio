Biochemical reaction networks

A biochemical reaction network represents interacting molecular reactions as a connected mathematical system.

A reaction may be written

\[A+B\xrightarrow{k}C.\]

Under mass-action kinetics its rate is proportional to the reactant concentrations:

\[v=k[A][B].\]

From reactions to ODEs

Each reaction contributes positive terms to species it produces and negative terms to species it consumes. Combining all reactions gives a system of differential equations.

Key idea. A reaction network converts a list of molecular interactions into a dynamical system that can be analysed mathematically.
