Enzyme kinetics

Enzyme kinetics studies how quickly enzyme-catalysed reactions proceed and how reaction rate depends on substrate concentration, enzyme concentration and kinetic parameters.

Basic reaction scheme

\[E+S \rightleftharpoons ES \rightarrow E+P.\]

The enzyme \(E\) binds substrate \(S\) to form the complex \(ES\), which can then produce product \(P\).

Why mathematical models are useful

They connect microscopic binding and conversion processes to measurable reaction rates and provide the foundation for Michaelis–Menten kinetics.

Key idea. Enzyme kinetics translates molecular reaction steps into equations for reaction rates and concentrations.
