Gene regulatory networks

A gene regulatory network contains several genes or molecular regulators that activate or inhibit one another.

A general model has the form

\[\frac{dx_i}{dt}=F_i(x_1,\ldots,x_n)-\delta_i x_i.\]

The function \(F_i\) describes how other components regulate production of component \(i\).

Feedback

Positive feedback can reinforce expression, while negative feedback can stabilise concentrations or generate oscillatory behaviour when combined with delays and nonlinear interactions.

Key idea. Network structure and feedback determine how interacting genes collectively behave through time.
