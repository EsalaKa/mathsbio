Equilibria

An equilibrium is a state that does not change with time under the model dynamics.

One-dimensional systems

For

\[\frac{dx}{dt}=f(x),\]

an equilibrium \(x^*\) satisfies

\[f(x^*)=0.\]

At this point the instantaneous rate of change is zero.

Systems of equations

For

\[\frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt}=\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x}),\]

an equilibrium \(\mathbf{x}^*\) satisfies

\[\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x}^*)=\mathbf{0}.\]

Biological meaning

An equilibrium may represent extinction, coexistence, a disease-free state, an endemic state, or another constant biological condition.

Key idea. An equilibrium identifies a possible steady state of the model, but whether nearby trajectories approach or move away from it is a separate question of stability.
