Enzyme kinetics

Enzyme kinetics studies the rates of biochemical reactions catalysed by enzymes. A basic reaction scheme is

\[E+S\;\rightleftharpoons\;ES\;\longrightarrow\;E+P,\]

where \(E\) is enzyme, \(S\) substrate, \(ES\) the enzyme–substrate complex and \(P\) product.

Mass-action modelling

If the rate constants are \(k_1,k_{-1},k_2\), mass-action kinetics gives

\[\frac{d[ES]}{dt}=k_1[E][S]-(k_{-1}+k_2)[ES].\]

The product formation rate is \(d[P]/dt=k_2[ES]\).

Key idea. Enzyme models translate molecular binding and conversion events into equations for changing concentrations.
