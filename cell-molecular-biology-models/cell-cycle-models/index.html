Cell-cycle models

The cell cycle is a regulated sequence of stages leading to cell division. Mathematical models describe transitions between stages and the molecular control systems that regulate them.

Stage-structured approach

Cells can be divided into states such as G1, S, G2 and M, with transition rates between states. Such models can be deterministic or stochastic.

Dynamical-systems approach

Models of cyclins, cyclin-dependent kinases and regulatory proteins use coupled nonlinear ODEs. Feedback loops can create switches and oscillations associated with cell-cycle transitions.

Population viewpoint

Cell-cycle structure also affects population growth because division times are distributed rather than identical across cells.

Key idea. Cell-cycle models connect molecular regulatory dynamics with progression through discrete biological stages and cell division.
