Biochemical reaction networks

A biochemical reaction network contains molecular species connected by reactions. Each reaction changes the numbers or concentrations of particular species.

Stoichiometric representation

With concentration vector \(x\), stoichiometric matrix \(N\), and reaction-rate vector \(v(x)\), a deterministic network can be written

\[\frac{dx}{dt}=Nv(x).\]

Each column of \(N\) records the net change caused by one reaction.

Modelling choices

Large molecular populations are often modelled by ODEs. When molecule numbers are small, reaction events may instead be represented by a continuous-time Markov chain.

Key idea. Reaction networks separate reaction structure, encoded by stoichiometry, from the kinetic laws controlling reaction rates.
