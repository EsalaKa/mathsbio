Cell signalling

Cell-signalling systems transmit information through molecular interactions, often beginning with receptor activation and continuing through a cascade of intracellular reactions.

Activation and deactivation

If \(X\) is inactive and \(X^*\) active, a simple model is

\[\frac{dX^*}{dt}=k_aS(X_T-X^*)-k_dX^*,\]

where \(S\) is an input signal, \(X_T\) the total amount, and \(k_a,k_d\) activation and deactivation constants.

Signal processing

Networks can amplify, filter, adapt to or integrate signals. Feedback and nonlinear response functions are therefore central to many signalling models.

Key idea. Mathematical signalling models describe how molecular networks transform an input into a time-dependent cellular response.
