Gene regulatory networks

A gene regulatory network describes interactions in which genes or their products activate or inhibit the expression of other genes.

Network model

For expression levels \(x_1,\ldots,x_n\), a general deterministic model is

\[\frac{dx_i}{dt}=F_i(x_1,\ldots,x_n)-\delta_i x_i.\]

The function \(F_i\) represents regulation of gene \(i\), while \(\delta_i\) represents degradation.

Feedback

Positive feedback can reinforce expression and may support multiple stable states. Negative feedback can stabilise concentrations or, with suitable delays and nonlinearities, contribute to oscillations.

Key idea. Regulatory-network behaviour emerges from the combined structure and strength of many activating and inhibitory interactions.
