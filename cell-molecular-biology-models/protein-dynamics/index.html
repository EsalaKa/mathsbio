Protein dynamics

Protein concentrations change through synthesis, degradation, transport and biochemical modification.

Basic production–degradation model

\[\frac{dP}{dt}=k_p m-\delta_pP,\]

where \(m\) is mRNA concentration, \(k_p\) is the translation rate and \(\delta_p\) is the protein degradation rate.

Coupled gene-expression model

\[\frac{dm}{dt}=\alpha-\delta_m m,\qquad \frac{dP}{dt}=k_pm-\delta_pP.\]

This simple system already introduces different timescales for mRNA and protein dynamics.

Key idea. Protein models connect gene expression to downstream molecular abundance through production and removal processes.
