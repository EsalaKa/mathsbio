Gene regulation

Gene regulation controls when and how strongly genes are expressed. Mathematical models often represent production together with molecular degradation.

Simple expression model

If \(m(t)\) is mRNA concentration,

\[\frac{dm}{dt}=\alpha-\delta m,\]

where \(\alpha\) is the production rate and \(\delta\) the degradation rate. The equilibrium is \(m^*=\alpha/\delta\).

Regulated production

Activation or repression can be represented by nonlinear functions. A common activation form is the Hill function

\[H(x)=\frac{x^n}{K^n+x^n}.\]

The parameter \(K\) sets a characteristic activation scale and \(n\) controls steepness.

Key idea. Gene-regulation models combine molecular production, degradation and regulatory feedback.
