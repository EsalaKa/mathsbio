← Cell and Molecular Biology Models

Intracellular stochasticity

Inside a cell, some molecular species occur in small numbers. Individual reaction events can therefore produce appreciable random fluctuations.

Stochastic reaction model

If reaction \(j\) has propensity \(a_j(x)\), then over a sufficiently small interval \(dt\),

\[P(\text{reaction }j\text{ occurs in }[t,t+dt))=a_j(x)dt+o(dt).\]

The resulting reaction network is a continuous-time Markov chain on molecular copy numbers.

Consequences of noise

Stochastic gene expression can create cell-to-cell variability even in genetically identical cells exposed to similar conditions. Noise may also influence switching between cellular states.

Simulation

The Gillespie algorithm can simulate individual reaction events exactly under the standard well-mixed Markov assumptions.

Key idea. At low molecular copy numbers, randomness is not merely measurement error; it can be an intrinsic part of intracellular dynamics.
