Evolutionary Mathematics

Study how inherited variation changes through populations and across generations. Begin with natural selection and population genetics, then develop Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium, mutation, genetic drift and classical finite-population models before moving to evolutionary game theory, replicator dynamics and adaptive evolution.
